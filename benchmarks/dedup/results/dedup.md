## Threats to validity

Carried over from the pre-registration, which states them in full:

- The `novel` label is GitHub's publication date, not the world's. See the list
  above for every item where that mattered.
- The coordinate-level `duplicate` label takes the version from the advisory's
  own OSV record, so OSV agreeing is close to a self-consistency check. What it
  does test is the as-of filter, the pagination accounting, and whether the
  second source agrees.
- OSV ingests GitHub advisories, so the two sources are not independent about a
  GHSA that OSV has already imported. They are independent about ingestion lag,
  withdrawals, and ecosystems only one of them carries.
- The as-of filter uses each record's current content with its original date, so
  an advisory amended after the cutoff is counted as it reads today. That
  direction inflates `CROWDED`.
- n is small. The intervals say so.

## What a CLEAR verdict here does not license

Nothing in this file is evidence that any finding is novel. A `CLEAR` verdict
means these specific corpora, asked these specific questions, at this specific
time, returned no rows. The duplicate that dominates real triage -- a finding
already sitting in the vendor's own queue -- is invisible to every corpus here.
That is why `coverage_confidence` is capped at 0.6 and carries its disclaimer as
a field rather than as prose somebody can drop.
